Every Friday, an employee left the store with a box of pastries. And every time, the guard opened the box.

Every time, it was just pastries! The guard would take one, eat it, and say: "Go on, enjoy!"

For three weeks now, a watch has gone missing every Friday!

Do you search everyone on the way out?

Everyone! Even the pastry boxes!

This time, he emptied the whole box… and still, nothing!

The watches were never in the box… they were on his wrist! The box was only there to make the guard look somewhere else.

Same mistake again: he was looking for something hidden!
