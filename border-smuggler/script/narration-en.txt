Once a month, this man crossed the border with a bicycle and a few sacks…

The guard was sure something was up! Every time, he emptied the sacks… but found nothing but sand.

The next month, the same man again, the same sacks… and sand once again! Each inspection got tougher, but he never found a thing.

But he never figured it out…

Because all along, he was searching the sacks for contraband… when the contraband was the bicycles themselves!
