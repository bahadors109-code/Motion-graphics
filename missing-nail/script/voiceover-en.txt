The gallery owner said his most valuable painting had been stolen… an old piece he had insured for a fortune.

They came in through here last night. They only took that painting.

When did you paint this wall?

Yesterday evening. Myself.

But that rectangle wasn't the mark of an old painting… it had just been made.

He had hidden the painting himself to collect the insurance… he just forgot that an imaginary painting would still need something to hang on!

A mark for the painting… but none for the nail.
