After the jewelry store robbery, a man said he saw everything from his window.

He was tall! Black coat, gloves… and a bag!

He ran down that alley!

I even saw him put a gold watch in his left pocket!

But one sentence made the officer stop writing…

You saw it from your window?

Yes, everything!

Then how did you see inside the shop through this shutter?

The witness knew the details a little too well… because during the robbery, he was on the other side of the shutter!

One detail too many ruined the whole story.
